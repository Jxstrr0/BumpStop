| 4 | 0.5.0.0 | The shop's three rigs, live: the shaker rig (four posts, slow to fast: finds where the body bounces and the wheels hop, and what washboard speed that is), the washboard ladder (now mostly rough gravel, a new ground, so it finds fade: every stock build fades first, checked by the balance bot) and the torture loop. | 6 | 0.7.0.0 | Sound and buzz, all made live in the browser (no recordings: none of the sound libraries were reachable, so there's nothing to credit yet). The lap runner counts hits for the sound without changing the run. How it works: `docs/SOUND.md`. |

| 5b | 0.7.2.0 | The 3D test track: each rig run now plays in 3D at the top of the live view.
