# Bump Stop: status

The plan of record is `docs/BUMP-STOP-PLAN.md`. Each PR adds a line here.

| PR | Version | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 0.1.0.0 | Repo scaffold copied from Mainline (TypeScript, Vite, three.js 0.187, Vitest, ESLint with the `src/sim/` bans), the deploy check and Pages deploy, release publishing, Dependabot, loading screen with the Longlight logo and © line, a one-screen preview of the proposed "Roadbook" look (Big Shoulders and Overpass Mono, self-hosted; magenta highlighter; the cool, warm, hot trace rule), the three classes as data, seeded RNG and deterministic maths, the plan in `docs/`, proposed names in `docs/NAMES.md` (English-flavoured north, Kelbo's pick). |
| 1 | 0.2.0.0 | Ride sim: the seven-part truck model (bounce, pitch, roll and four wheels) at 500 Hz, with springs, progressive bump stops, limit straps, anti-roll bars and tires that can leave the ground; a test mule for each class; seeded ground recipes (graded, washboard, whoops, rocks, jumps, frost heaves); damper heat from the damper's own work, fade from hot oil and foaming, stiff cold oil; long stages from full-detail samples plus exact heating and cooling in between (a 300 km stage in about half a second); tests for determinism, save-and-carry-on, energy, sampling against full detail, and the purity bans (now also any `Date` and `crypto`). The preview page's sample trace is now a live test stage you can run for each class. How it works: `docs/RIDE-SIM.md`. |
| 2 | 0.3.0.0 | Wear and breaks: six failures, each from its own cause and with its own trace (damper fade from the oil over its red line, bottoming from metal-to-metal hits, cracked mounts and frame zones from fatigue, snapped axles and CVs from torque against strength at the joint's angle, worst landing on a spinning wheel, and binding past its biggest angle, cut tires from squashing to the rim over a sharp edge); test-mule parts for each class; a rolling 3-second trace of every channel, cut out per failure when something breaks; where-to-fix lists for the report; lap runs that get 5 km/h faster each lap (first drafts of the washboard ladder and torture loop); wear on long stages, scaled from the samples like heat; the balance bot, which checks every class in CI (each test mule lasts a few laps then breaks inside its speed window, each failure can be made to happen first, and its fix helps) and writes a table to the Actions summary. The preview page has a break test you can run for each class. How it works: `docs/WEAR.md`. |
| 3a | 0.3.1.0 | Parts and the truck builder: twelve slots (frame, front and rear suspension, dampers, bump stops, anti-roll bars, axles, wheels, tires, protection, engine, spares) with parts for all three classes, each with mass, cost in antlers and the year it comes out; per-axle settings (spring rate, preload, damper clicks, bump stops, limit straps, tire pressure, adjustable bars); ride height built from the spring's sag, so softer springs lose bump travel and the joint angle follows; tire stiffness from tire pressure; the rules (weight window, test budget, year); a stock build per class that drives like its test mule. The balance bot now drives the stock builds. How it works: `docs/BUILD.md`. |
| 3b | 0.4.0.0 | The build screen: pick the class and the year, a part for every slot (with what each one adds or saves in kilograms and antlers, and parts not out yet greyed with their year), and tune each axle with sliders (spring rate, preload, bump stops, limit strap, tire pressure, and damper clicks and bar positions where the parts have them), with a line showing where the truck sits in its travel. Weight against the class window and money against the test budget stay pinned at the top, with what keeps the build out of a raid. The ride sim and the break test now drive the truck on the build screen. Builds live in memory until saves arrive (plan PR 8). |
| 4 | 0.5.0.0 | The shop's three rigs, live: the shaker rig (four posts, slow to fast: finds where the body bounces and the wheels hop, and what washboard speed that is), the washboard ladder (now mostly rough gravel, a new ground, so it finds fade: every stock build fades first, checked by the balance bot) and the torture loop. Each shows the lap, its speed and ground, each corner's oil, travel and wear with their words, and a live trace of the run, with Fast, Skip to the end and Stop. The report: what let go, where and when in one sentence, why, a crew chief's note, the last 3 seconds in slow motion with Normal, Warm and Over the limit bands and chips for each channel, up to three fixes that jump to the right part or slider (front or rear), and a bar to run it again. Ghost runs: the last run on each rig shows as a faint dashed line and the report says Better, Same or Worse. Traces now end on the step that broke. The old test benches are gone. How it works: `docs/RIGS.md`. |
| 5a | 0.5.1.0 | The 3D trucks: one Blender script (`scripts/blender/make-trucks.py`) builds all three classes from a shared kit of parts (wheels with tread, shocks with magenta coil springs, arms, solid axles, tubes, lamps) with each class's own body on top: a lifted pickup for the light 4x4, a cab-over rally truck with a service box for the heavy truck, a tube-cage buggy for the side-by-side. Every moving part is its own named node, and a test checks the wheels sit where the sim's test mules have them. The build screen shows your truck in 3D on the shop floor, at its own ride height (softer springs or less preload and that end squats), turning slowly; drag sideways to turn it. three.js and the models only load when the view comes on screen. How it works: `docs/3D.md`. |
